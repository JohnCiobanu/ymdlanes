01 Equipment

Capacity for what you ship.

Full-truckload service on three trailer types, so one carrier can cover more of your freight.

Dry Van

53' enclosed trailers for palletized and floor-loaded general freight. Clean, sealed and secure from dock to dock.

  • General freight
  • Palletized
  • Retail & CPG

Reefer

Temperature-controlled trailers for fresh, frozen and climate-sensitive loads, with temps monitored for the whole haul.

  • Frozen
  • Fresh & produce
  • Temp-sensitive

Flatbed & Step Deck

Open-deck capacity for steel, metal sheet, building materials and machinery. Properly secured, and tarped when needed.

  • Steel & metal
  • Building materials
  • Machinery
